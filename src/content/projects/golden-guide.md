Agentic AI assistant built for QHacks’ “Golden Age” theme. Golden Guide helps Kingston’s elderly residents navigate municipal services — not just by answering questions, but by taking action: searching programs, checking eligibility, looking up transit, drafting emails, and offering real phone calls (with confirmation first).
